The difference

How The Roost is different

Anyone can read about this work and pass a quiz on it — then freeze the first time a real ticket lands. The Roost is built so that can’t happen here.

01

Roost Practicals gate every certificate

Every certification is two parts: a knowledge exam and a hands-on practical under exam conditions — fix the broken rack, contain the incident, build the program to spec while the machine checks your work. Multiple choice is only the backup singer. Pass both halves or no certificate.

02

A tutor that never leaves you stuck

Every room carries three levels of help — a nudge, a stronger hint, then a full plain-language walkthrough. Nobody hits a dead end and quits. And the less help you take, the more XP the work earns.

03

Real-life Missions

Rooms teach skills; Missions test whether you can do the job. Work a simulated SOC shift — a phishing email, a 2 A.M. brute-force alert, a boss asking what happened — and get an End of Shift Report scored on Detection, Response, Communication, and Prioritization that sends you straight back to the room that trains your weak spot. Every attempt re-rolls the details, so nobody memorizes a score.

04

Mock interviews with a hard rule

Sit a real interview for SOC Analyst, Help Desk, Junior Network Technician, Junior Penetration Tester, or Junior Developer — answered in your own words and scored. The rule: “Job-Ready” requires hands-on evidence — a passed Practical or a strong mission score. Talk a perfect game with no doing on your record and you cap at “Nearly There,” with a plan that sends you to the machines.

05

Certificates that hold up

Passing issues a certificate with your name, the date, and a unique credential ID — plus a public verify page where an employer or a school can confirm it’s real, and the exact fields to drop into LinkedIn’s “Add certification.” Every certificate states it plainly: earned by doing — includes hands-on performance assessment.

Origin

Where it all started

Our founder learned to program on a Commodore 64. No accounts, no downloads, no forty-minute setup video — you typed PRINT, you typed RUN, and the machine did the thing. Instantly. That first RUN is the hook that makes a programmer.

So The Roost opens with that same blue screen — READY. — and a new generation starts the way he did. Kids begin there, grow through Python and the Code Labs, and can end up certified for real security jobs on the very same platform.

The front door nobody else built: fall in love with making the machine obey you first. The career comes after.

Straight answers

Questions, answered honestly

No marketing fog. If there’s a limit to what The Roost is, we say it here first.

Is it free?

Beginner paths are free, full stop — free from the first room to the last, not free until the interesting part. That’s written into Our Pledge above, along with no lab timers, no mid-path padlocks, and one honest price for everything beyond the free paths. And when paid plans arrive, every update stays included in the one plan.

Do I need experience?

None. The First Flight Check places you at your real level, and the Commodore 64 path starts at absolute zero — a blue screen, a READY. prompt, and your first line of code in the first minute. If you can type, you can start.

Are the certificates real?

They are The Roost’s own training credentials — earned by passing a knowledge exam and a hands-on practical, issued with a unique credential ID that anyone can check on our public verify page. Each certificate also names the industry exam it readies you for — Foundations and Blue Team point to CompTIA Security+, Networking to Network+ and CCST. Only CompTIA issues CompTIA’s certificate, and we say so right on ours. Our job is to deliver you to that exam door ready.
